04 — Video surveillance

One of the biggest headaches, sorted

Video surveillance is one of the areas where companies most often break the rules without realising. We make sure your cameras comply: information signs in the monitored area, a full privacy notice, handling of the rights of the people filmed, control of the maximum retention period and answers to any questions that come up.
