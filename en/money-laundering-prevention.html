Money laundering prevention

Adaptation to Law 10/2010 on the Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing, in two versions depending on the size of the obliged entity.

Get in touch 900 929 806
Two options

Depending on your headcount and turnover

We have developed two levels of advice, so that what we do matches what each type of non-financial obliged entity has to comply with.

AML Diligence

Fewer than 10 employees and less than €2M turnover

  • Initial analysis of the client's situation.
  • Appointment of the representative before SEPBLAC, the Spanish anti-money laundering authority.
  • Drafting of the internal money laundering prevention policy.
  • Annual audit.
  • Response to requests and inspections from SEPBLAC.
  • Ongoing advice on money laundering and terrorist financing prevention.
AML Integral

More than 10 employees and more than €2M turnover

  • Everything included in the Diligence service.
  • Setting up the Internal Control Body (OCIC), where necessary.
  • Drafting of the Procedures Manual.
  • Preparation of the Annual Training Plan.
  • Annual review by an external expert.
  • Legal advice, legal defence and civil liability insurance.

A good reason to be properly advised. Failing to comply with the Law on the Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing carries penalties that can range from €60,000 to €1,500,000, or higher amounts depending on the infringement.

Obliged entities

Does it apply to you?

The law lists who is subject to these obligations. It also covers non-resident persons and entities that carry out the same kind of activity in Spain through branches, agents or services provided without a permanent establishment.

If you are not sure whether you are on the list, tell us and we will look into it: that is the first question to answer.
